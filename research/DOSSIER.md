### What the map represents

There are several different kinds of history here: documented secret operations; religious and communal movements; occult and spiritualist institutions; places where conspiracy stories were told; and archives where claims can be investigated. Their presence on one map does not establish a common network. A group’s unusual theology does not by itself establish coercion or criminal conduct.

**Evidence language:** Strong means direct, relevant documentation, with independent support where appropriate. Moderate means useful documentation with important limits. Limited means a thin or unresolved part of a claim. These labels apply to the stated association, not every belief or story attached to a site. Official organizations establish their addresses and advertised activities; their websites are not independent validation of spiritual or medical claims.

**Coordinates:** approximate historical-property or address positions. Some current organizations, large properties and demolished buildings do not have a precise surviving event location. These are context pins, not verified trailheads or permission to enter. Historical associations are better established than present-day access at several sites.

### Jolly West: two reliable pins, one unresolved proposal

H29 marks the UCLA Neuropsychiatric Institute’s institutional context at 760 Westwood Plaza. H30 marks research access to West’s papers at UCLA Library Special Collections. Neither identifies an experiment room. The current Semel building description establishes the address; it does not establish West’s exact historic office.

A 1974 *Science for the People* article reproduces language from a January 1973 West letter proposing use of a Nike base in the Santa Monica Mountains for work associated with the proposed violence center. A contemporary *Black Panther* issue also discusses the proposal. These are useful contemporary sources, including critical perspectives; the original letter and complete proposal should be examined before stronger claims are made. **I have not established which base was meant. H25 must not be relabeled as that facility.** The UCLA finding aid describes the center’s plans as unrealized. Start archival work with Series 6.4 (CSRV), 6.6 (University of Oklahoma), and 7.2 (Cults), then identify relevant dated folders. A proposal is different from a completed or operating program.

Read the [1974 contemporary critique](https://archive.scienceforthepeople.org/vol-6/v6n3/violence-center/) and [contemporary newspaper discussion](https://www.lincoln.edu/_files/langston-hughes-memorial-library/Black%20Panthers%20Newspaper%20Publications/BP02021974.pdf). A [CIA-hosted 1977 press clipping](https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP88-01315R000400160008-6.pdf) discusses West’s earlier Oklahoma LSD research. That is a newspaper clipping retained in CIA files, not an operational finding about experiments at UCLA.

*CHAOS* is a useful investigative reading, to be compared with the cited documents. O’Neill’s [congressional testimony](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119hhrg64141/pdf/CHRG-119hhrg64141.pdf) records his claims; publication as testimony does not make them congressional findings. The reviewed evidence does not establish that West directed Manson or that a specific UCLA building housed the operations alleged in later theories.

### Suggested reading sessions

1. **Jolly West, institutions and documentary gaps:** *CHAOS*, the UCLA finding aid, the 1974 critique and the 1977 clipping. Ask which statements are documents, witness accounts, interpretations or missing links.
2. **Rocket science and occultism:** George Pendle’s *Strange Angel*, then the Parsonage history and PRS. A shared person or social circle is a verifiable connection; a coordinated intelligence operation needs additional evidence.
3. **Ascended masters and Cold War apocalypse:** the I AM chronology, the Critical Dictionary article on CUT/TSL, and Erin Prophet’s *Prophet’s Daughter*. The scholarly article describes historical links between Theosophy, the I AM milieu and later Summit Lighthouse teachings. Its author is also Elizabeth Clare Prophet’s daughter, an important source-position disclosure.
4. **How institutions tell their histories:** Lawrence Wright’s *Going Clear*, the Celebrity Centre’s own description and the CCHR museum’s own presentation. Compare source selection and the treatment of critics.
5. **Real secrecy and accountability:** Daniel Ellsberg’s *Secrets*, the Fielding break-in records, Lookout Mountain and DOE’s SSFL documents. Keep documented events separate from speculative extensions.
6. **How conspiracy stories become places:** the 1934 Lizard People article, the military history of the Battle of Los Angeles, and the McMartin case and tunnel analysis.

**Best broad overview for this project:** Erik Davis’s *The Visionary State*, with photographs by Michael Rauner. Suggested books below have verified bibliographic links; this dossier does not claim that their full contents were independently checked.
