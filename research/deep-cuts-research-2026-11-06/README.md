# Research supplement for local Codex

This is an add-on to `conspiracy-book-club-codex-handoff.zip`, not a replacement.

Copy this folder into your local atlas project's `research/` directory. It adds eight new research threads and updates existing leads L11–L14. It leaves the 72-place publication snapshot, spreadsheet, and map unchanged.

- `RESEARCH.md`: findings, evidence strength, unresolved points, sources, readings, and next actions.
- `findings.csv`: 12 structured research records. Coordinates are blank and publication flags are false. Reference URLs include both sources consulted and explicitly identified follow-up leads; the report records the difference.
