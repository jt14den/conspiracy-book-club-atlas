# Conspiracy Book Club and Social Aid and Pleasure Club
## A Southern California reading shelf

Prepared November 6, 2026. Los Angeles, the valleys, Orange County and Ventura County, with a prospective San Diego County shelf.

[Open the club’s live map](https://conspiracy-book-club-los-angeles.bracedhawk1.chatgpt.site/)

**Start with the six books below.** The rest are choices for particular places and interests, not a homework assignment. The selection favors books with a clear connection to the map and gives memoirs, historical research, investigative arguments and fiction distinct roles. Place pairings come from the map dossier and its linked records; they do not imply every book discusses every named property.

Bibliographic details and subject scope were checked against publisher, author, library and catalog records. Available excerpts and author notes informed the selection; these are not full-text reviews of every book. Prices, stock, audiobook editions and local borrowing availability have not been checked.

## Start here: six books, in this order

1. **[The Visionary State: A Journey Through California’s Spiritual Landscape — Erik Davis, photographs by Michael Rauner](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL3404595M/The_visionary_state).**
   **Role:** Regional overview and photographic history. **Pair with:** PRS, Hollywood and Ojai Krotona, and the wider spiritual landscape. The closest single companion to this project. Its broad approach includes architecture and religious experimentation, rather than treating every unusual institution as a criminal organization. Davis’s [Hollywood tour notes](https://techgnosis.com/visionary-hollywood-tour/) are a useful short companion.

2. **[Strange Angel: The Otherworldly Life of Rocket Scientist John Whiteside Parsons — George Pendle](https://www.hachette.co.uk/titles/george-pendle/strange-angel/9781399606646/).**
   **Role:** Biography and history of science. **Pair with:** The former Parsonage in Pasadena. A particularly good match for the club’s overlap of rocket science, occult practice and Southern California social worlds. Read for documented people and relationships; a striking association does not automatically establish an intelligence operation.

3. **[Orange Sunshine: The Brotherhood of Eternal Love and Its Quest to Spread Peace, Love, and Acid to the World — Nicholas Schou](https://us.macmillan.com/books/9780312607173/orangesunshine/).**
   **Role:** Investigative history. **Pair with:** Former Mystic Arts World, Laguna Beach; optional Sandpiper stop. The focused Orange County selection for the Brotherhood’s spiritual ambitions, counterculture and drug distribution. The publisher provides an opening excerpt.

4. **[American Trickster: The Hidden Lives of Carlos Castaneda — Ru Marshall](https://orbooks.com/catalog/american-trickster/).**
   **Role:** Critical biography, published in 2026. **Pair with:** Castaneda’s former Westwood compound and the Fowler collection. Start here for a recent reconstruction of Castaneda’s life and circle, then compare it with Amy Wallace’s memoir below. A [published excerpt](https://crimereads.com/ru-marshall-on-the-curious-lives-and-legends-of-carlos-castaneda/) offers a sample of the biographer’s approach.

5. **[The Search for the “Manchurian Candidate”: The CIA and Mind Control — John Marks](https://wwnorton.co.uk/books/9780393307948-the-search-for-the-manchurian-candidate).**
   **Role:** Documentary investigative history. **Pair with:** The Jolly West research thread, rather than a claimed experiment room. Use this to establish the broader CIA mind-control program before taking up proposed connections to Manson. It supplies historical grounding, not proof of every later local theory.

6. **[CHAOS: Charles Manson, the CIA, and the Secret History of the Sixties — Tom O’Neill with Dan Piepenbring](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/tom-oneill/chaos/9780316477574/).**
   **Role:** Investigative argument. **Pair with:** Spahn Ranch and the UCLA West papers. Probably the liveliest discussion choice for the club’s conspiracy interests. Read with the records beside it: distinguish documents, witness recollections, unresolved questions and the author’s inferences. The map research has not established that West directed Manson.

## Choose a thread

### Castaneda, occult Los Angeles and Hollywood

7. **[Sorcerer’s Apprentice: My Life with Carlos Castaneda — Amy Wallace](https://www.northatlanticbooks.com/shop/sorcerers-apprentice/).**
   **Role:** Participant memoir. **Pair with:** The Westwood compound. Wallace’s account of her relationship with Castaneda and experiences in his circle makes a useful partner to Marshall’s biography. A firsthand perspective can illuminate personal dynamics while retaining the limits of memory and position.

8. **[Master of the Mysteries — Louis Sahagun](https://books.apple.com/us/book/master-of-the-mysteries/id1109770906).**
   **Role:** Reported biography. **Pair with:** Philosophical Research Society. The book follows Manly Palmer Hall and LA’s esoteric world. The expanded edition is subtitled *New Revelations on the Life of Manly Palmer Hall*; earlier editions use *The Life of Manly Palmer Hall*. Choose it for a PRS outing and the history behind the library.

9. **[The Source: The Untold Story of Father Yod, Ya Ho Wa 13, and the Source Family — Isis Aquarian and Electricity Aquarian](https://www.thesedaysla.com/products/the-source-the-untold-story-of-father-yod-yahowa13-and-the-source-family).**
   **Role:** Insider history and archival account. **Pair with:** The former Source restaurant on Sunset. A route into communal life, music and the group’s own image-making. The authors were members; treat that access and allegiance as part of the source. [The documentary’s official account](https://thesourcedoc.com/about) confirms the book’s relationship to its participant archive.

10. **[Going Clear: Scientology, Hollywood, and the Prison of Belief — Lawrence Wright](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/212925/going-clear-by-lawrence-wright/9780307745309/).**
    **Role:** Investigative history. **Pair with:** Celebrity Centre and the Valley campus; CCHR as a separate institutional presentation. A Hollywood-focused companion for questions about celebrity, organizational power and belief. A controversy described in the book should not be assigned to a particular campus without its own evidence.

### Communal movements and lives inside them

11. **[The Rise and Fall of Synanon: A California Utopia — Rod Janzen](https://www.press.jhu.edu/books/title/2826/rise-and-fall-synanon).**
    **Role:** Historical study using primary sources and interviews. **Pair with:** Casa del Mar’s former Synanon use. A direct choice for the movement’s transformation from rehabilitation program to commune and religion. The 2023 edition has a new preface. A useful comparison with the fictional rehabilitation institution in *A Scanner Darkly*.

12. **[Prophet’s Daughter: My Life with Elizabeth Clare Prophet inside the Church Universal and Triumphant — Erin Prophet](https://discover.bklynlibrary.org/item?b=11430344).**
    **Role:** Family and participant memoir. **Pair with:** King Gillette Ranch’s former Camelot chapter. A Cold War and apocalyptic reading through the leader’s daughter. The memoir’s wider story reaches beyond California; the later Montana shelter preparations should not be relocated to the ranch. [Author interview](https://dianerehm.org/shows/2008-10-20/erin-prophet-prophets-daughter-lyons-press/).

13. **[Sex Cult Nun: Breaking Away from the Children of God, a Wild, Radical Religious Cult — Faith Jones](https://www.sexcultnun.com/).**
    **Role:** Survivor and family memoir. **Pair with:** The Huntington Beach Light Club as a movement-history connection. Jones is David Berg’s granddaughter. Her childhood account concerns the movement’s later international life, not a firsthand reconstruction of the 1968 Huntington Beach venue. This is the more demanding selection for sexual abuse and coercion.

### Ojai, Theosophy and the contemplative alternative

14. **[Star in the East: Krishnamurti, the Invention of a Messiah — Roland Vernon](https://www.simonandschuster.com/books/Star-in-the-East/Roland-Vernon/9780971078680).**
    **Role:** Biography. **Pair with:** The Krishnamurti Center and the wider Krotona/Ojai history. Use it to explore the contrast between an organization’s expectations of a teacher and that teacher’s later rejection of spiritual authority. Pair it with Krishnamurti’s own 1929 speech below.

15. **[The Perennial Philosophy — Aldous Huxley](https://digitalcommons.rockefeller.edu/jason-brown-library/78/).**
    **Role:** Primary philosophical writing and comparative mystical anthology. **Pair with:** Former Trabuco College / Ramakrishna Monastery. It offers the intellectual vocabulary behind one strand of Southern California’s spiritual experimentation. Read it as Huxley’s interpretation, not a neutral account proving that all traditions mean the same thing. The monastery’s [institutional history](https://www.ramakrishnamonastery.org/about) describes his use of its library.

### State secrecy, political history and moral panic

16. **[Secrets: A Memoir of Vietnam and the Pentagon Papers — Daniel Ellsberg](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/288995/secrets-by-daniel-ellsberg/).**
    **Role:** Participant memoir of documented secrecy and disclosure. **Pair with:** Former RAND headquarters and a separate Chez Jay stop. The most direct book for the Pentagon Papers thread. The bar’s specific handoff story needs its own corroboration; the memoir’s relevance does not authenticate every piece of local lore.

17. **[Satan’s Silence: Ritual Abuse and the Making of a Modern American Witch Hunt — Debbie Nathan and Michael R. Snedeker](https://search.worldcat.org/title/Satan%27s-silence-%3A-ritual-abuse-and-the-making-of-a-modern-American-witch-hunt/oclc/32589966).**
    **Role:** Critical investigation of ritual-abuse prosecutions and moral panic. **Pair with:** Former McMartin Preschool. A counterpoint to the temptation to treat allegations as discoveries. The authors argue a position; compare their reconstruction with the relevant case records rather than treating any single book as a legal finding.

18. **[Robert Kennedy: His Life — Evan Thomas](https://www.simonandschuster.net/books/Robert-Kennedy/Evan-Thomas/9781476734569).**
    **Role:** Political biography. **Pair with:** The Ambassador Hotel site, already checked off. This is context for Kennedy and the political stakes of 1968, rather than a book selected to prove an assassination conspiracy.

## Two fiction nights

19. **A Scanner Darkly — Philip K. Dick.**
    **Pair with:** Orange County and the Pollak Library papers; Synanon as a comparative discussion. Drugs, surveillance, divided identity and a rehabilitation institution make it a particularly apt club selection. The fictional institution is not a documentary description of a specific map property.

20. **VALIS — Philip K. Dick.**
    **Pair with:** The Fullerton research thread. Choose this for revelation, interpretation and the experience of finding meaning in patterns. It belongs on the fiction shelf, even where it draws on Dick’s experiences.

## San Diego County: three additions to the shelf

These extend the reading list. The ten candidate stops below have not yet been added to the live map.

21. **[Heaven’s Gate: America’s UFO Religion — Benjamin E. Zeller](https://nyupress.org/9781479881062/heavens-gate/).**
    **Role:** Scholarly historical study. **Pair with:** The former Rancho Santa Fe site. My first San Diego selection: it examines the movement’s development, beliefs and social world, rather than reducing it to its final catastrophe.

22. **[California Utopia: Point Loma, 1897–1942 — Emmett A. Greenwalt](https://sandiegohistory.org/journal/1979/july/br-utopia/feed/).**
    **Role:** Historical study. **Pair with:** Former Lomaland, now the Point Loma Nazarene University campus. Useful for the Theosophical community and its educational experiment. An earlier version appeared as *The Point Loma Community in California, 1897–1942: A Theosophical Experiment*. Compare it with [SDSU’s archive-based exhibition](https://library.sdsu.edu/scua/exhibits/revisiting-visionary-utopia-lomaland).

23. **[Autobiography of a Yogi — Paramahansa Yogananda](https://yogananda.org/autobiography-of-a-yogi).**
    **Role:** Religious autobiography and primary teaching text. **Pair with:** Encinitas Hermitage and gardens. Read the Encinitas chapter for the direct local connection. Its miraculous and spiritual claims are part of the author’s account, not independently verified effects. The [original edition is available through Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/7452/7452-h/7452-h.htm).

## A short document packet to keep beside the books

- **Jolly West and the proposed violence center:** [Contemporary 1974 critique in Science for the People](https://archive.scienceforthepeople.org/vol-6/v6n3/violence-center/). A critical contemporary account; examine the underlying original documents before making stronger claims. The map does not identify a verified West experiment room or a particular operating Nike-base facility.
- **Castaneda’s collecting:** [Fowler Museum’s 2014 exhibition record](https://fowler.ucla.edu/exhibitions/fowler-in-focus-the-yaqui-masks-of-carlos-castaneda/). Evidence of collecting should be kept distinct from authentication of the don Juan narratives. The exhibition has ended.
- **Krishnamurti and spiritual authority:** [Dissolution of the Order of the Star, August 3, 1929](https://kfoundation.org/dissolution-speech/). The speech was delivered in the Netherlands, not at the Ojai campus.
- **Ellsberg and disclosure:** [Ellsberg’s Pentagon Papers material](https://www.ellsberg.net/pentagon-papers/).
- **Theosophy in San Diego:** [SDSU’s Lomaland exhibition](https://library.sdsu.edu/scua/exhibits/revisiting-visionary-utopia-lomaland) and the [San Diego History Center collection](https://sandiegohistory.org/collection/photographs/lomaland/).
